简体中文

如何开始？

先在“人物”页添加至少两个人物，再到“关系”页建立关系。图谱页会用圆环、等级、星球或星云方式展示已保存的数据。

数据保存在哪里？

数据先保存在当前设备；iCloud 可用时，通过你的私有 CloudKit 数据库同步到登录同一 Apple 账户的设备。无需独立应用账号，离线仍可使用。请继续定期导出 JSON 备份。

换设备或重新安装前需要做什么？

先等待 iCloud 有机会同步，并额外导出备份保存到可信位置。重新安装或更换设备后，同一 Apple 账户的数据会自动出现；必要时也可在“设置”中导入备份，导入会在确认后替换当前数据。

如何报告问题？

请说明设备型号、系统版本、应用版本、复现步骤和期望结果；不要在邮件中附上包含真实人物信息的备份文件，除非你已确认并愿意分享。

English

How do I get started?

Add at least two people on the People screen, then create a connection on the Relationships screen. Atlas can display saved data in ring, hierarchy, planet, or nebula layouts.

Where is my data stored?

Data is stored on this device first. When iCloud is available, your private CloudKit database synchronizes it to devices signed in to the same Apple Account. No separate app account is required, and the app remains usable offline. Continue exporting JSON backups regularly.

What should I do before reinstalling or changing devices?

Allow iCloud time to synchronize, and also export a backup to a trusted location. Data for the same Apple Account should appear automatically after reinstalling or changing devices; you can import the backup if needed. Import replaces current data after confirmation.

How do I report a problem?

Include the device model, system version, app version, steps to reproduce, and expected result. Do not attach a backup containing real personal information unless you have reviewed it and intend to share it.
